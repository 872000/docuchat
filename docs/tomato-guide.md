# Tomato Growing Guide

## Watering

Tomatoes need consistent watering. Water deeply two to three times per week, giving each plant about 2 to 3 litres per watering. Water at the base of the plant in the morning so the leaves stay dry, which helps prevent fungal disease. In hot weather above 30 degrees, water every day.

## Sunlight and soil

Tomatoes need at least 6 to 8 hours of direct sunlight per day. Plant them in well drained soil rich in compost, with a pH between 6.0 and 6.8. Space plants about 60 centimetres apart so air can circulate.

## Feeding

Feed tomato plants every two weeks with a balanced fertilizer once the first flowers appear. Stop feeding about two weeks before you expect to harvest the last fruit.

## Common problems

Blossom end rot shows up as a dark sunken patch on the bottom of the fruit and is caused by uneven watering or low calcium. Keep watering consistent and add crushed eggshells or garden lime to the soil to help prevent it.

Aphids cluster on new growth and can be washed off with a strong jet of water. For a heavier infestation, spray the leaves with soapy water in the evening.
