# Acme Widgets — Customer Support Handbook

## Returns and refunds

You can return any unused product within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. To start a return, email support@acmewidgets.example with your order number and the reason for the return. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of us receiving the returned item.

Damaged or defective items are replaced free of charge. If your order arrives damaged, photograph the damage and contact support within 7 days of delivery so we can ship a replacement right away.

## Shipping

Orders placed before 2pm ship the same business day. Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and is free for orders over $50. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $12.95.

## Warranty

Every Acme widget comes with a 2 year warranty covering manufacturing defects. The warranty does not cover damage from misuse, drops, or water exposure.

## Contact

Our support team is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm Eastern. Email support@acmewidgets.example or call 1-800-555-0142.
